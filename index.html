WELCOME TO THE CAGE

CAGE KINGS
STREET FOOTBALL

Fast five-a-side football. Rule the street, score goals, and become a Cage King.

HOW TO PLAY

WIN THE CAGE

Score more goals than Red Block FC in five minutes.

Each team has five players: one goalkeeper and four outfield players.

After every goal, the team that conceded must restart with a pass from the centre.

CONTROLS

TAKE CONTROL

PC: WASD or arrows to move · E to switch player

PC: P pass · Space shoot · T tackle · Q strong tackle · Shift sprint

ANDROID: use the left stick to move, then tap RUN, PASS, SHOOT, or TACKLE.

Tap a Blue player on the pitch to control them.

YOUR TEAM

FIVE CAGE KINGS

1 KEEPER: protects the goal and restarts play.

2–5 OUTFIELD: pass, dribble, tackle, and score.

All players begin with equal ability. Future training improves each player separately.

DRIBBLING: move close to the ball. Sprint carefully, pass to teammates in space, and shoot near goal.
